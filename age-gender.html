How it works

The face detector + neural network estimate each face. Gender is reported as male or female only — that's all the model can output. It's guessing from appearance, so it can say nothing about anyone's actual identity or sexuality.

Does anything throw it off? Yes — glasses (especially sunglasses), hats, beards, poor lighting, and tilted faces reduce accuracy, so take any result as a rough guess.

Why not nationality?

This app deliberately does not try to guess nationality or ethnicity. Decades of research show appearance cannot reliably determine someone’s nationality — people are far more diverse than any label. Any attempt would be inaccurate and harmful, and that’s not what a face does.

First run downloads ~2 MB of model weights from a CDN (cached afterwards). Needs internet on first load; needs Firefox/Chrome/Edge for webcam access.
